About DSSSBPYQ.Online

Welcome to DSSSBPYQ.Online, the premier open-access preparation hub for Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) TGT Computer Science and General Ability examinations.

Our goal is to provide high-quality practice test series, previous year papers (PYQs) with detailed solutions, topic-wise booster modules, and complete syllabus tracking tools completely free of charge to all computer science teaching aspirants.

Platform Features

  • Full CBT Mock Tests: Timed 200-question practice tests with real exam marking scheme (+1, -0.25).
  • Part A General Ability: Comprehensive mocks for General Awareness, Reasoning, Math, English, and Hindi.
  • Part B Computer Science: Coverage of Operating Systems, DBMS, Networking, Data Structures, Programming, and Teaching Methodology.
  • Syllabus Tracker: Interactive syllabus completion tracker across 32 domain topics.
